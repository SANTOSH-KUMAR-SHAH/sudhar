# 22. SERVICE REQUEST STATES

The teacher's conceptual lifecycle is:

### Initial
**Requested**

Customer can withdraw/cancel under the defined rules.

### Normal flow
**Requested → Assigned → Confirmed → Technician On Way → Diagnosing → In Progress**

### Repair delay
**In Progress → Waiting for Parts → In Progress**

### Final
**In Progress → Completed → Invoiced → Paid → Closed**

The exact final state machine can be refined, but every state should represent a meaningful real-world condition.

# 26. WAITING FOR PARTS

Real repair work can stop because a part is unavailable.

Therefore:

```text
In Progress
    ↓
Waiting for Parts
    ↓
Part Available
    ↓
In Progress
```

This is a real operational state, not just an edge case.

Future parts tracking may include:

- part
- quantity
- availability
- source
- cost
- expected arrival
- notes
- customer communication

Exact inventory architecture is not finalized.

# 27. INVOICE + PAYMENT

The teacher's model intentionally separates:

**Completed → Invoiced → Paid → Closed**

This means:

### Completed
Technical work is finished.

### Invoiced
The amount owed is formally recorded.

### Paid
Customer payment is received.

### Closed
The service case is finalized.

Do not collapse all four into one "completed" status.
